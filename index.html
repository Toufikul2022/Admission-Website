Questions

Something else? Write to us — a person replies within two working days.

Is it really free?

Yes. Classes, mock tests, notes and mentoring cost nothing. We're funded by alumni and partner schools, and we never ask for card details.

Can I take both LCAT and MACAT?

One track per intake, so you get full mentor time. You can switch tracks in the first two weeks.

What do I need to join?

A phone or laptop, a stable connection for live classes, and about eight hours a week.

Do I need to pass a test to get in?

No. The starter mock only tells your mentor where to begin.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes — complete 80% of classes and all mocks to receive one.
